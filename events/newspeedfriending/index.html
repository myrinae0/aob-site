what?

🎉
speedfriending

this is a monthly community event where we get together to make friends!

at the start of the event, attendees will get paired up with another member for 5 or so minutes. you can talk about whatever you want but sometimes we’ll have a theme for the event if you need a conversation starter. then we switch and repeat!

towards the end of the event, we’ll have some free time to talk to anyone you didn’t get paired up with or want to talk to again. we also invite everyone to grab some boba or food together afterwards!
