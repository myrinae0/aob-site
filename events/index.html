💬 i have more questions about an event!

each event has a page that should have some more details, including where and when. you can also take a look at the tags in case you want to get an idea of what the event is about. if you have more a question that isn’t answered there, please send us a DM (contacts in the footer) and we’d be happy to answer!

‣
☕ if i have an idea for an event, how do i tell you / can i host one?

that’s great to hear! we’re so happy when our community members host events!

if you’d like some help planning / gauging interest, feel free to reach out on any platform but discord often works best.

‣
☀️ i’m hosting an event and want to let people know about it, can i get it posted here?

we’re very open to the idea! we have a few rough guidelines on events that we share, but please send the info our way in any case!

  • in and around the boston area
  • asian or at asian adjacent topics
    • this is a very, very wide breath of events
  • volunteering opportunities
