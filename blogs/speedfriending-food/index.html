speedfriending - food

food!

Each speedfriending session we’re introducing a theme as a conversation topic. It’s not mandatory to talk about it but we think that it helps our members if they can come in with some thoughts and ease into a flow! Building on our last topic, we chose food as the focus for this session.

“Do you cook Asian food?” ★ “Do you feel closer to your culture by cooking its cuisine?” ★ “How did you learn to cook Asian dishes?” “What’s your go-to simple dish to make? When will you make it?” “What’s the most complex dish you’ve had to make? Why was it hard?”

“Where do you eat Asian food?” “Do you get to eat Asian food at home? School? Work?” ★ “What is your best spot for Asian food in Boston?” “Have you eaten Asian dishes in their origin country? How does it differ?”

“What is your favorite Asian…?” “Candy?” ★ “Dish?” “Drink?” “Dessert?”

“How do you feel about…?” “... bringing Asian food into ‘non-Asian’ spaces ?” ★ “... the Asian food scene in Boston?” ★ “... ‘Asian fusion’ restaurants?”
