where do these posts come from?

from our wonderful, vibrant community!

we often ask members questions that range from thought provoking to downright silly, and everything in between.

sometimes we specifically ask folks to share stuff about themselves!

it’s our hope that folks can find hope, a sense of belonging, or just have a good laugh when reading these posts!

‣
can i publish something here too?

absolutely! send us a message over at hello@asiansofboston.com, or any of our other platforms, and we can talk specifics.

relevant topics include but aren’t limited to:

  • personal stories
  • life in boston
  • asian-american news
  • performances, festivals, rallies, etc.
‣
i’m in a post and i don’t want to be, can you remove it?

for sure! send us a message over at hello@asiansofboston.com, or any of our other platforms, and we can talk specifics.

we definitely try to make clear when/how we publish these posts, so let us know how we’re doing.
